# VectorXR 0.19.0 - Pivot Snap Stacking, Position Assist, and Steadier Turbo

Lets Pivot's Enhanced Motion keep working inside Snap Views, adds Position Assist and soft deadzones, keeps games running when the runtime rejects a Turbo frame, adds a Debug flight recorder for long-session problems, and makes Quadviews eye tracking more dependable.

## Pivot

- New **Apply Enhanced Motion to Snap Views**: a motion profile can keep its rotation assistance and Position Assist working around a selected Snap View for the same application. Motion profiles without it keep Snap Views exclusive, as before.
- New **Position Assist** adds sideways lean, height, and forward/back movement as you turn, with start and full-offset angles, Gentle and Strong starting points, and optional per-direction tuning. It stays level and follows the snapped view.
- New **Deadzone Transition** eases sensitivity in beyond the deadzone instead of switching at its edge, for all directions or each direction separately.
- Instant (0 s) Snap View and nudge transitions apply on the next frame, keeping both eyes and tracked controllers in step.
- Snap View profiles no longer take part in activation or Always Active, and a control shared by an activation, a Snap View, and a nudge triggers only one of them. Only the profile that owns the nudges, the selected Snap View or the engaged motion profile, responds to nudge input.
- The OSD shows Snap View state on a second Pivot line when the application has Snap Views.

## Turbo

- Runtime frame errors no longer stop the game: Turbo re-establishes the frame on the game's own frame thread and continues with Sequenced pacing for the session. Recovered faults appear in the Turbo Safety fault log and do not block Turbo.
- Async pacing on runtimes that reject early frame calls, such as Windows Mixed Reality, now waits for a late runtime frame instead of dropping it with an error.
- Ending a VR session, or a game resizing its render targets, can no longer hang on a pending Turbo frame.

## Diagnostics

- With **Log Level: Debug**, a flight recorder keeps recent frame-call history and freezes snapshots of stalls, faults, and recoveries, so problems that appear after a long session can be reported with **Export Debug**.
- Export Debug keeps every session's main log ahead of older recorder history.

## Quadviews

- Eye tracking works alongside games that set up their own eye-tracking bindings, recovers after the headset loses and regains focus, and keeps working when a game recreates its VR session. The log now explains why gaze is unavailable when it is.
- Focus Resolution goes up to 300% and both resolutions adjust in 1% steps. Above 200% the app explains the extra GPU memory and compositing cost.
