# VectorXR 0.18.0 - In-Headset OSD, Sharper Pivot Views, and Turbo Timing Controls

Adds a customizable in-headset performance display, fixes Pivot's eye-tracked foveation alignment in MSFS 2024, introduces per-profile Turbo timing strategies, and makes Turbo and controller bindings more dependable.

## In-headset OSD

- New **On-Screen Display** page adds an optional head-following panel showing application FPS, frame times, a frame-time graph, Turbo state, Pivot, and active enhancements.
- Works in Direct3D 11, Direct3D 12, and Vulkan games, including Quadviews sessions.
- Choose a detailed or compact layout, arrange its rows, and set placement, size, opacity, accent color, and a 12- or 24-hour clock. Save your favorite layouts as presets.
- Position the panel with a live desktop preview, or pop the preview out into its own window while you adjust settings.
- Press Ctrl+Alt+F10 to show or hide it and Ctrl+Alt+F11 to switch layouts. Both shortcuts can be reassigned to keyboard, joystick, or hat inputs.
- Known issue: on Pimax headsets, the panel's edges can show slight color fringing, except in DCS with Quadviews.

## Pivot

- Fixes the high-resolution region drifting away from your gaze when Pivot turns the view in MSFS 2024 with eye-tracked foveated rendering.
- Physical leaning now follows the rotated view, including combined yaw and pitch, and Snap View transitions pass smoothly through 180 degrees.
- Keeps your seated position correct after you reset or recenter your view in SteamVR, Oculus, or Pimax.

## Turbo

- Adds **Toolkit-style** and **Custom** timing strategies to custom profiles, with advanced prediction and frame-cap controls for experimentation. Performance Diagnostics labels each capture with the strategy it used.
- Switching Turbo off in game keeps frames in order, avoiding the errors and freezes some Async setups hit at that moment.
- When Turbo Safety stops Turbo, or Turbo is disabled while a game is running, frame pacing settles immediately instead of stuttering until the game restarts.
- Shows restart requirements and the active timing choice more clearly in the desktop app and OSD.
- Pausing a Turbo performance capture now saves everything captured so far.

## Controllers and bindings

- Connects joysticks and HOTAS devices in the background, removing periodic frame-time spikes when a bound device is missing or reconnecting.
- A switch that is already on when its device connects no longer toggles Turbo, Pivot, or the OSD by itself.

## Setup

- The OpenXR Layers page recognizes when VectorXR is running as administrator and applies machine-wide layer changes without an extra permission prompt.
- Adds collapsible setup guides to the Depth and Quadviews pages and clearer descriptions across the enhancement pages.
