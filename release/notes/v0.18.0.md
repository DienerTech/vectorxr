## In-Headset OSD and Sharper Pivot Views

Adds a customizable in-headset performance display, fixes Pivot's eye-tracked foveation alignment in MSFS 2024, and expands per-profile Turbo timing controls.

### Changes

- <strong>In-headset OSD</strong>
  - Adds an optional headset display for application FPS, frame times, Turbo state, Pivot, and active enhancements in D3D11, D3D12, and Vulkan sessions.
  - Choose compact or detailed layouts, arrange status rows, customize placement and colors, and save reusable presets with a desktop preview.
- <strong>Pivot</strong>
  - Fixes the high-resolution region drifting away from your gaze when Pivot turns the view in MSFS 2024 with eye-tracked foveated rendering enabled.
  - Preserves eye direction relative to the headset while retaining Pivot's camera and controller transformations.
  - Keeps physical leaning aligned with the rotated view, including combined yaw and pitch, and improves Snap View transitions around 180 degrees.
- <strong>Turbo</strong>
  - Adds Toolkit-style and Custom timing strategies to custom profiles, with advanced prediction and frame-cap controls for experimentation.
  - Makes restart requirements and active timing choices clearer in the desktop app and OSD.
- <strong>Setup and reliability</strong>
  - Improves setup guidance and descriptions across the enhancement pages.
  - Adds regression coverage for eye-gaze bindings, controller behavior, and action-space lifetimes.
