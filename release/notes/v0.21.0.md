# VectorXR 0.21.0 - Screenshots, Stabilized Mirror, and App Navigation

Save full-resolution screenshots from the headset with a key or button, and open a stabilized desktop mirror for streaming and recording. The sidebar is regrouped by task, and a new Supporters page thanks the community. Frame Generation's Auto rate handles hitches, loading screens and games near the refresh rate better, shader flow shows fewer specks, and a range of stability fixes come with it.

## Capture & Mirror

- **Screenshots.** Assign a keyboard or device binding to save one or both eyes as full-resolution PNGs in Direct3D 11 and Direct3D 12 games. Files are saved in the background, and presses in quick succession are all saved.
- **Stabilized Mirror.** A desktop window that shows one eye with head rotation smoothed, for streaming, recording, or a spectator display. The headset view stays the same. Works in Direct3D 11, Direct3D 12, and Vulkan games; turn it on before launching a Vulkan game. A mirror that fills a monitor lets mouse clicks through to the game.
- **With Quadviews.** Where the runtime or Frame Generation composes quad views, the mirror and screenshots draw the sharp focus view over the surrounding view in Direct3D 11 games.

## App

- **Grouped sidebar.** Capture & Mirror has a page for each utility, Registry and Discovery have their own pages under Applications, and Pivot sits with Depth under Look and Feel. The sidebar remembers which groups you collapse.
- **Guides** for Screenshots and the Stabilized Mirror, with walkthroughs and optional music. Setup guides and notes on each page start collapsed.
- **Supporters.** A new page thanks the Patreon members who help fund VectorXR, by tier and with the names they chose to share, and the wider community for its donations, reports and ideas.
- **Safer imports.** Network paths for screenshots and binding sounds are cleared from an imported settings file and listed in the preview.
- Turbo's experimental **Sample prediction timing at wait entry** option is removed: it had no effect. Profiles that set it still load.

## Frame Generation

- **Lowest rate for Auto.** With Fixed Auto, set **Lowest rate** to 2x to keep Frame Generation on for games that run just under the refresh rate and stutter each time it stands by.
- **Auto** starts afresh when Frame Generation is turned back on, ignores frames from loading screens and mode switches, engages below 90% of the refresh rate as Dynamic does, and checks the game's GPU time before trying full rate. On SteamVR with Pimax headsets it now engages for games running between refresh steps (for example 51 fps at 90 Hz).
- **Fewer specks with shader flow** in flat areas such as sky and water. The default Artifact suppression is now 10; profiles keep the value they have.
- **Half rate explained.** When the runtime holds frames to half rate, the On-Screen Display names the setting to turn off (for example, Smart Smoothing in Pimax Play) and counts down to the next try.
- Auto falls back from NVIDIA hardware flow only when the flow itself runs late.
- Less video memory in games that send no depth, such as DCS.

## Fixes

- With Turbo on, leaving VR could hang the game if the runtime was still holding a frame wait at that moment.
- Frame Generation retries output images the runtime is slow to return and presents only complete image sets. Deferred swapchain work is bounded while the game acquires its images.
- Fixed several rare stalls and device losses in the Direct3D 11 and Vulkan bridges, and a race between waits on the Direct3D 12 queue. On AMD and Intel GPUs the bridges check that images can be shared before using them.
- Direct3D 11 rendering and copies are no longer affected by the game's conditional rendering.
- Turbo keeps its timing across mode handoffs, and the experimental frame cap now averages its target.
- The Quadviews overlay stays off in a session the game began in stereo.
- Quadviews focus sharpening no longer clips HDR highlights. Extend head prediction gives every request for one display time the same head position.
- Settings the app does not recognize, such as those a newer version wrote, are kept when you save, and Artifact suppression and Motion limit are kept in range so a hand-edited value cannot make the layer reject the settings file. The log names the VectorXR edition and version at the top.

## Known issues

- Screenshots are not available in Vulkan games yet. The mirror and screenshots show the focus view of composed quad views in Direct3D 11 games only.
- On SteamVR with Pimax headsets, Auto may not engage for a game held at exactly half the refresh rate.
- In helicopters, Interpolate can show artifacts around the rotors and in the sky behind them.
- On Varjo headsets with Quadviews, focus sharpening is off in DCS while Frame Generation runs.
- Turbo prevents runtime reprojection such as SteamVR Motion Smoothing from working. Use one or the other.
